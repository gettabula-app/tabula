BFavicon monogram

Tabula, in sixteen pixels.

Four options, each pure SVG with no web font. The 180 is the Bodoni Moda outline; 16, 32 and 48 are redrawn on the pixel grid so the hairlines land on whole pixels.

  1. A

    Full stopInk T, red square, on light paper

    The wordmark, shortened. The T and the red square, kerned in under its arm the way a full stop tucks under a T. The most direct link to "Tabula." and the quietest in a tab row. On dark chrome it shows as a light tile.

Recommendation

D, Stamp.

At 16 px it is the clearest of the four: a solid red tile with a light T is one shape, and it reads the same in light and dark chrome and on every site ground. The paper and ink options need their tile edge to read on a matching chrome; Stamp never does.

It is also the most Tabula: the red square from the wordmark, at full size, with the T cut out, as the collage cuts everything else. A, Full stop, is the safer second choice if the mark should stay closer to the wordmark.

For Johan

Four choices, then the build.

Pick these and the full build follows: the dark tokens in tokens.json, the dark guide, and the ICO and PNG set.

  1. Ship a dark variant?

    Yes or not yet. If yes, it follows the OS setting, with a switch on the page.

  2. Ink or Night cobalt.

    Ink keeps the palette as it is. Night cobalt sits closer to the brand blue, but adds four new colours.

  3. Which monogram.

    A, B, C or D. The recommendation is D, Stamp. One may also be paired, such as D for the favicon and A for avatars.

  4. The wordmark square in dark.

    Keep the brand red #D42A18 (shown here), or use red-hi #FF5B47 like the other marks.
