# Licence for the Tabula brand asset kit

**Open question for Johan: the licence is not decided yet.** The proposal is CC BY 4.0 for the art. Until it is decided, treat the kit as "all rights reserved, Tabula".

## What is in the kit

- `cutouts/`, `marks/`, `paper/`, `tape/`, `board/`: original images generated with Codex (OpenAI image generation) for the Tabula landing page (TAB-72, #24 and #27), then cut out, keyed and compressed for TAB-196. No stock photos, no third-party artwork, no real people.
- `wordmark/`: the Tabula wordmark. The letters are outlines of Bodoni Moda ExtraBold (SIL Open Font License 1.1, which allows outlining glyphs in a logo). The red square is part of the mark.
- `torn-edges.svg`, `torn-edges.css`: the torn-paper clip paths. Code, same licence as the Tabula repository.

## Proposal

- Art (cutouts, marks, paper, tape, board): CC BY 4.0, attribution "Tabula".
- Wordmark: not under CC. The name and mark stay Tabula's. Others may use them to refer to Tabula, unchanged, as described in docs/brand.md.
