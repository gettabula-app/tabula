02What is on the board

Everything you need to cut, paste and connect.

Notes, shapes and lines that stay put, and a room that stays in sync.

  • 01

    Sticky notes

    Notes and shapes on a snapping grid. Drop, drag, tidy.

  • 02

    Attached connectors

    Lines follow the notes they join, however you rearrange them.

  • 03

    Diagrams

    UML and flowchart shapes, icons, and Mermaid import.

  • 04

    Live presence

    Cursors, selections and who is here, synced by a small relay.

  • 05

    Offline first

    Every board lives in your browser first and merges later.

  • 06

    Comment threads

    Talk on the board itself, right next to the note you mean.

  • 07

    Five themes

    Default, Ayu, Kanagawa, Matrix and Evergreen.

  • 08

    Teams and roles

    Owner, admin, member, guest. Editor, commenter, viewer.
