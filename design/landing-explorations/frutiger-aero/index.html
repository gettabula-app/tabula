Local-first whiteboard

A clear sky for your team's best thinking

Tabula is an infinite whiteboard with sticky notes, connectors that stay attached and facilitated exercises. Every board lives in your browser first, so it works offline and syncs live when your team is around.

Everything a workshop needs, nothing in the way

Draw, group and run exercises on one canvas, with your team right there.

Sticky notes and shapes

Notes, shapes on a snapping grid, flowchart and UML shapes, icons, and Mermaid import for diagrams you already have.

Connectors that stay put

Link ideas and move them around. Connectors stay attached to their shapes, however much you rearrange.

Facilitated sessions

Guide the room through session steps with timers, and keep everyone on the same part of the board.

Live and offline

See cursors, selections and presence in real time. Lose your connection and keep working; changes merge later.

Private writing

Everyone writes first with notes hidden, then you reveal together. Less anchoring, more honest answers.

Dot voting and polls

Decide as a group with dot voting and quick polls, and keep the discussion in comment threads.

Your board, in your browser first

Tabula keeps every board on your device and uses a small relay to sync when it can. No spinner between you and your idea.

Run a session in three steps

From a blank frame to a decision, with the room moving together.

1

Set the steps

Lay out the session as steps with timers, then guide the group from one to the next.

2

Write privately

Everyone adds notes that stay hidden until you reveal them all at once.

3

Vote and decide

Cluster the ideas, dot vote, run a poll and leave comments on what comes next.
