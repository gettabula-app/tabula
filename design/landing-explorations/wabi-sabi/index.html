What it holds

Few tools, kept well.

Everything a team needs to gather thoughts, and nothing that asks for attention.

Notes, frames, connectors

Sticky notes and shapes snap to a grid. Connectors stay attached when you move things. Flowchart and UML shapes, Mermaid import, and icons are there when you need them.

Sessions with a pace

Step through an exercise with timers. Write privately and reveal together, vote with dots, run a poll, and keep discussion in comment threads.

Yours first, shared when you are

  • Every board lives in your browser and works offline.
  • A small relay syncs cursors, selections and presence when reachable, and merges later.
  • Workspaces with teams and roles: owner, admin, member, guest.
