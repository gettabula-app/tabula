Everything a workshop needs on the wall

Draw, group and connect on a snapping grid, then run the session without leaving the board.

Sticky notes and shapes

Notes, frames and shapes snap to a grid. Add UML and flowchart shapes, icons, or import a Mermaid diagram.

Connectors that stay attached

Link any two things. Move one and the arrow follows, so your map never comes apart.

Session steps with timers

Walk the group through steps, each with its own countdown, so every exercise ends on time.

Private writing, then reveal

Everyone writes without seeing the others. Notes stay hidden until the facilitator reveals them.

Dot voting and polls

Give each person a set of dots, or ask a quick poll. Comment threads keep the discussion next to the idea.

Local first, live when you can

Every board lives in your browser and works offline. A small relay shows cursors and presence, and merges changes later.

Run the room, not the tool

A facilitated session in three moves. You set the steps; Tabula keeps everyone in the same one.

Write alone

A timer starts. Each person adds notes that only they can see.

Reveal and group

Notes appear for everyone at once. Drag similar ones into a frame.

Vote and decide

Spend your dots on what matters most and leave with a ranked board.
