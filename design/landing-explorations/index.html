Proposals for the public landing page, one art style each. Each is a complete static page: hero, how it works, templates, pricing teaser, call to action and footer. The app itself keeps its Swiss direction. This page updates by itself as proposals are ready.

    Ben-Day dots, thick comic outlines, primaries and hot pink, speech bubbles.
  • 23Pointillism

  • 24Mixed Media
