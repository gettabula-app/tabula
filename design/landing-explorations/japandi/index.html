Features

Everything a workshop needs, nothing it does not.

Notes, shapes and connectors

Sticky notes and shapes snap to a grid. Connectors stay attached when you move things. UML and flowchart shapes, icons, and Mermaid import for diagrams you already have.

Facilitated sessions

Run a workshop in steps, each with a timer. Write privately, then dot vote, run a poll and talk it through in comment threads.

Teams and roles

Workspaces with owners, admins, members and guests. Per board, people can edit, comment or view. Sign in with an email link.

Five themes

Default, Ayu, Kanagawa, Matrix and Evergreen. Choose the one that is easy on your eyes during a long retro.

Offline first

Your board opens before the network does.

Every board lives in your browser first and works offline.

A small relay syncs live cursors, selections and presence when it can be reached, and merges everything later when it cannot.
