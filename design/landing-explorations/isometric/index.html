Everything a working session needs, on one board.

Notes, shapes and connectors that stay put

Drop sticky notes into frames, snap shapes to a grid and link them with connectors that stay attached when you move things.

  • UML and flowchart shapes
  • Mermaid import
  • Icons and comment threads

Run the session, not just the board

Step through an agenda with timers. Let everyone write in private, reveal together, then settle it with dot voting or a quick poll.

  • Session steps with timers
  • Notes hidden until reveal
  • Dot voting and polls

Local first, live when you can be

Your board opens instantly and keeps working offline. A small relay shares cursors, selections and presence, then merges everyone's changes later.

  • Works offline in the browser
  • Live cursors and presence
  • Teams, roles and email sign-in links
