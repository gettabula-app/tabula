Follow one session down the page

1

Invite the team in

Share a link or sign people in by email. Roles decide who edits, comments or just watches, and everyone's cursor shows up live.

Owner, admin, member, guest
2

Everyone writes, nobody peeks

Set a timer and start the step. Notes stay hidden until the facilitator reveals them, so the first idea does not steer the room.

Private writing and timers
3

Reveal and cluster

Flip every note at once, then drag similar ideas together. Add shapes, connectors that stay attached, and comment threads where the talking happens.

Sticky notes, shapes, comments
4

Vote with dots

Give everyone a handful of dots and let them spend them on what matters. Need a quick yes or no? Run a poll.

Dot voting and polls
5

Decide, then keep the board

The winner is clear, the discussion is attached to it, and the board stays in your browser, offline included. Sync merges whenever the relay is reachable.

Local first, live when connected
