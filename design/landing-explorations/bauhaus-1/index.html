Everything a session needs

Eight tools, one board. Grouped by where you meet them.

  • On the board
  • With your team
  • Yours to keep

  • Sticky notes

    Notes and shapes on a snapping grid. Drop, drag, tidy.

  • Attached connectors

    Lines follow the notes they join, however you rearrange.

  • Diagrams

    UML and flowchart shapes, icons, and Mermaid import.

  • Live presence

    Cursors, selections and who is here, plus comment threads.

  • Timed steps

    Build a session from steps with timers and run it for the room.

  • Workspaces and roles

    Teams, owners, admins, members and guests. Editor, commenter or viewer per board.

  • Five themes

    Default, Ayu, Kanagawa, Matrix and Evergreen.

  • Open source

    Read the code, self-host with Docker, or use hosted workspaces.
