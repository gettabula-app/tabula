1

Boards live in your browser

The board is on your machine first. Planes, trains and bad hotel wifi are not an incident.

  • Works offline, always.
  • A small relay syncs live when reachable: cursors, selections, presence.
  • Offline edits merge later.
ref 1.0 / storage
2

Objects that behave

An infinite canvas with a snapping grid, so the mess is at least aligned.

  • Sticky notes and shapes on a snapping grid.
  • Connectors that stay attached when you move things.
  • UML and flowchart shapes, plus icons.
  • Mermaid import: paste text, get a diagram.

3

Run the meeting from the board

Facilitation is built in, not bolted on.

  • Session steps with timers.
  • Private writing: notes stay hidden until the reveal.
  • Dot voting and polls.
  • Comment threads on anything.
ref 3.0 / sessions
4

Workspaces and roles

Teams get a home. Sign in with an email link, no password to forget.

  • Roles: owner admin member guest.
  • Per board: editor, commenter or viewer.
  • Hosted workspaces live at <slug>.gettabula.app.
ref 4.0 / access
5

Your board, your colours

Five themes. Pick the one that matches your terminal or your mood.

  • Default, Ayu, Kanagawa, Matrix, Evergreen.
  • Self-host with Docker, or let us host it.
