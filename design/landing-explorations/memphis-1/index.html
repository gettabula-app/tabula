Run the room, not just the canvas

Sticky notes, shapes and connectors are all here. What sets Tabula apart is the facilitation built around them.

Write privately, reveal together

Everyone writes at once and notes stay hidden until you reveal them. Step through the session with timers so nobody anchors on the loudest voice.

Vote with dots, ask with polls

Hand out dot votes, run a quick poll and keep the discussion in comment threads right on the note it is about.

Local first, live when you can be

Every board opens from your browser, even offline. When the relay is reachable, cursors, selections and presence sync live, and changes merge later.
