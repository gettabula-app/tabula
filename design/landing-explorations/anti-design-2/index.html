A local-first infinite whiteboard for teams. Sticky notes, shapes, connectors and facilitated exercises. The board lives in your browser and works offline. Sync is a bonus, not a requirement.

01what.it.does

It's a board. It's also a meeting.

01.1Canvas

  • Sticky notes and shapes on a snapping grid.
  • Connectors that stay attached.
  • UML and flowchart shapes, icons.
  • Mermaid import.

01.2Facilitation

  • Session steps with timers.
  • Private writing, notes hidden until reveal.
  • Dot voting and polls.
  • Comment threads.

01.3Local first, synced second

  • Every board lives in your browser and works offline.
  • A small relay adds live cursors, selections and presence when reachable, and merges later.

01.4Teams

  • Workspaces with teams and roles: owneradminmemberguest
  • Board access: editor, commenter, viewer.
  • Email sign-in links. Five themes: Default, Ayu, Kanagawa, Matrix, Evergreen.
