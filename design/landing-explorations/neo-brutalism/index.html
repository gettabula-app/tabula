01 Features

One canvas.
No ceremony.

Everything a team needs to get ideas out, group them and agree, without a setup wizard in sight.

  • Notes, shapes, connectors

    Sticky notes and shapes snap to a grid. Connectors stay attached when you move things. UML and flowchart shapes included, plus Mermaid import and icons.

  • Facilitated sessions

    Step through a session with timers. Write privately, with notes hidden until the reveal. Then dot vote, run polls and discuss in comment threads.

  • Offline first, live when it can

    Every board lives in your browser and works offline. A small relay shares cursors, selections and presence, and merges changes later.

  • Open source, your server

    Self-host with Docker or use a hosted workspace. Teams, roles, and email sign-in links. Five themes, so it fits your eyes.

02 How it works

Three moves
to a decision.

Pick a frame, run the room, keep what matters. The board is the record.

  1. Open a board

    Start blank or from one of 14 templates. It is on your screen instantly, nothing to install and no account needed to begin.

  2. Run the session

    Set a timer, let everyone write in private, reveal together, then dot vote. Cursors show who is where.

  3. Sync when you can

    Work on a train, on a plane, in a basement. Reconnect and the relay merges everyone's changes.
