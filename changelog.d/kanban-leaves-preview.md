section: Changed

- Kanban boards are available to everyone who can edit a board. The `?kanban` address switch is gone: the Kanban tool and **Make kanban from selection** show for everyone, a sticky can become a loose card, and a paste, a duplicate, an imported board file and a template keep kanbans like any other object. The kanban templates are in the Templates page and the Templates drawer. Existing kanbans are unchanged.
