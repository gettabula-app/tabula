- A hosted instance keeps the report of the last finished hour (the same scrubbed report the Privacy tab previews, built in the last minute of each hour and handed out only once that hour has ended; a build that crosses the boundary is discarded, and no capture runs during a restore) and hands it to the control plane at `GET /api/internal/telemetry`, with the control token only. It answers 204 when `TABULA_TELEMETRY=off`, 503 until an hour has finished (`no_report_yet`), when a capture was missed (`stale_report`) or while a restore runs, and keeps answering 200 with operating data only after an owner opt-out of product analytics. Release labels such as `2026.10.09-1` are normalised to the report schema, `data_bytes` is measured in the background, and refused control-token attempts on internal routes are throttled (429 past 30 a minute) and logged once a minute without any credential. The usage push now carries `telemetryAnalytics` (the owner's choice) and is sent at once when the switch changes. The instance itself still sends nothing. A workspace id that does not fit the report schema is now logged once at start and named in the Privacy preview, instead of leaving the route at 503 for ever.
