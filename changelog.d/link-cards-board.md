- Link cards on the board (client, part 1): paste or drop a web address, or use the new Link button on the rail, to put a card on the board that fills in its title, description and image from the server's preview. The card has compact, card and large layouts, shows pending, failed and off states, opens its address with Enter, a double click or Cmd/Ctrl+click, and asks first when an address looks like it holds a secret. It appears only where the server fetches previews.
