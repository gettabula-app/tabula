section: Changed

- Starting a dot vote from the toolbar now asks what can be voted on: the selected items (the default when something is selected, and the only way to vote on a frame), every note, shape, card, text and image as before, or sticky notes only, with a count of the items. Shift-click on the button, or "Start on everything", starts at once as before. While a vote runs, the items that can take a dot have a faint dashed outline, and a click on anything else says it is not part of the vote.
