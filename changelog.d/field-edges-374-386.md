section: Changed

- Text fields, text areas, selects, search fields and tab controls have a flat bottom line that starts and stops at the corner radius, instead of a dark edge that curled up the sides. A focused field gets a 2px ring all round, and an icon search field carries its ring on the field itself (TAB-374).
  A chosen row in the font picker, option lists and chat lists shows a straight accent bar that stops short of the row's corners (TAB-386). The email field on the sign-in page fills the column like its label and button (TAB-376).
