- Texts have resize handles (TAB-233, `docs/guide/shapes-text-notes.md`). A selected text shows handles on its left and right sides, which change the width it wraps at (its height follows its lines), and on its four corners, which scale the type and the width together from the opposite corner, so the lines keep breaking where they did. A finger reaches the handles from further away and sees them larger. From the keyboard, `Alt`+`Shift`+`Left` or `Right` changes the width and `Alt`+`Shift`+`Up` or `Down` changes the size, one undo step each press, announced.
