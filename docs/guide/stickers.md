# Stickers

Stickers are colour emoji you place on a board, for reactions and decoration. They are stored in the board, so placed stickers work offline and appear in exports.

## Open the Stickers drawer

Click **Stickers** on the tool rail. The drawer opens on the side.

- **Set** chooses the emoji style: **Fluent**, **Twemoji** or **Noto**. Fluent is the default.
- **Search stickers** filters the current set. Results appear after a short pause.
- A note under the grid names the licence. Some sets need attribution if you publish your board.

## Place a sticker

- Click a sticker to add it at the centre of your view.
- Or drag it onto the board where you want it.

A sticker is larger than a normal icon so it reads at a glance. Select it to move, resize (it keeps its proportions), rotate, duplicate, lock or delete it, like any other object. You can also put one inside a frame.

Stickers have no colour control. The colour comes from the emoji. The properties panel is titled **Sticker** for a selected sticker.

## Reactions

To react to something on the board:

1. Select the note, shape or other object.
2. Click **React with a sticker** in the quick-action bar.
3. Pick one of the 16 reactions, such as a thumbs up, heart, party popper, eyes or fire.

A small sticker is placed beside the top right corner of your selection. It does not follow the object if you move it later, and it has no counter. Delete it like any object, or undo.

## Offline

- Stickers already on the board always display, including in PNG and SVG exports.
- Browsing and searching the drawer needs a connection. Sets and previews you have viewed before may still load offline.
- A reaction you have never used on this device cannot load offline. You will see a message that the icon could not be loaded.
- If a search fails, the drawer says why and shows **Retry**. It also retries when your connection returns.
