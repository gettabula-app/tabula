# Lines

A line is a plain stroke on the board. It is not attached to anything: use it for dividers, underlines, arrows in a sketch, or a path you want to place by eye. For a line that stays attached to shapes, use a [connector](connectors.md) instead.

## Draw a line

Pick **Line** in the **Connectors and lines** group of the left rail, or press `Shift+L`. Then either:

- drag from where the line should start to where it should end, or
- click once for the start, and click again for the end.

The tool returns to **Select** when the line is drawn. Hold `Shift` while you draw to snap the line to 15-degree steps. Hold `Cmd` (`Ctrl` on Windows and Linux) to lock it horizontal or vertical.

## Move or reshape a line

Select a line and drag either end to move that end; the other end stays where it is. A line's ends stay free: dragging an end over a shape does not attach it. For a line that stays attached, draw a connector.

## Style a line

Select a line and open **More properties** (the quick-action bar has the colour only):

- **Stroke colour**
- **Width**
- **Style** (solid, dashed or dotted)
- **Start** and **End** arrowheads, from the same list of heads that connectors use. New lines have no arrowheads.
- **Reverse direction**, which swaps the start and the end (so an arrowhead points the other way).
- **Opacity**

## Lines and connectors

A line and a connector look alike, but they behave differently:

- A **line** is free. It has two points on the board and does not follow any shape.
- A **connector** is attached to shapes, sticky notes or other objects at one or both ends, and moves with them.
