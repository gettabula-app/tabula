# Keyboard and screen readers

You can use most of Tabula without a mouse, and a screen reader hears what changes on the board and what is on it. This page covers what works, and what does not yet.

## Moving around

- `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` move through the toolbars, panels and menus. A ring shows where you are.
- Dialogs keep `Tab` inside until you close them, and the board behind them is not reachable. `Esc` closes a dialog or a menu and puts you back on the button that opened it.
- Menus and panels that open from a button start on their first item.
- In a group of choices, such as the colours in a swatch row, alignment, or the themes in the board menu, `Tab` stops once on the chosen option. The arrow keys, `Home` and `End` move between options and choose one. For the dots-per-person presets and the sticky note colours, the arrow keys move and `Space` or `Enter` chooses.
- Every tool has a shortcut. See [Shapes, text and sticky notes](shapes-text-notes.md#the-tool-rail) and [Getting started](getting-started.md).

## What a screen reader announces

Buttons and fields have names, colours are named ("Blue", not a code), and each dialog is named by its heading. The board page has a main area, labelled regions for the top bars and the quick-action bar, and a heading with the board's name.

A hidden announcer says what you would otherwise only see:

- People joining and leaving.
- Changes of the sync state, such as **Live** or **No access**.
- Objects deleted or added (including by duplicate or paste), undo and redo, and moving an object forward or backward.
- New comments and replies from other people.
- A session timer starting, pausing and running out, and your dots left after you stop clicking.
- Poll results being revealed. The "answered" count on a poll updates in place.

Messages that appear on screen for a few seconds, such as **Locked**, are announced too. The sign-in email and team name fields are marked invalid when they have an error.

## Other settings

- Tabula follows your system setting for reduced motion: slides and fades are shortened or removed.
- In a high contrast mode, colour swatches keep their colours and the chosen option is outlined.
- You can zoom the page with your browser. On a phone, pinching the toolbars zooms the page. Pinching the board itself zooms the board.

## The board from the keyboard

`Tab` goes into the board once, and the next `Tab` leaves it, so you never have to press `Tab` once for each object. Inside the board, a visible ring marks the current object, and a screen reader says what it is (a note, a frame, a picture or a connector, its words and its place in the list).

- Arrow keys move to the nearest object in that direction.
- `Page Down` and `Page Up` move to the next and previous object in reading order, and `Home` and `End` go to the first and last.
- Nothing on the board changes while you move around.

## What does not work yet

You cannot yet select, move, resize or connect existing objects with the keyboard; selecting and editing from the keyboard come next. You can create objects with the tool shortcuts and, once something is selected, nudge it with the arrow keys, edit its text with `Enter`, duplicate, delete and change its stacking.
